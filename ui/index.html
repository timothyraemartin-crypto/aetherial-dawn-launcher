GAME VERSION

Match the server's Skyrim

  1. Steam's console has opened. If it didn't, press Windows+R, type steam://open/console and press Enter.
  2. Copy each line below, paste it into the box at the bottom of Steam's console and press Enter. Steam downloads them one after another.
  3. When the console has said Depot download complete for every line, click Install.
  • Steam does the download with your own account. The launcher never sees your Steam password.
  • The download is up to about 15 GB. Your saves and mods stay.
  • In Steam, set Skyrim to Only update this game when I launch it, and start the game from this launcher.
